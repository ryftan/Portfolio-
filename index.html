A bit about the work.

Hi, I'm Ryftan. I write and design game narratives out of Lyon, France, under the name Dusk Harbor. Mostly I build the boring-but-essential stuff — the frameworks and docs that stop a story falling apart once five people start writing in it.

How I actually work.

The framework I designed for Olympos Games' narrative department — four governing pillars, and the 14-phase pipeline behind every document that follows.

P1
The World Remembers

Player actions alter relationships and environments, and echo later — even the small ones.

P2
Emotional Ambiguity

No good/evil binaries — characters carry contradictions the player has to sit with.

P3
Discovery Through Observation

The story rarely over-explains itself — the player investigates, rather than gets told.

P4
Narrative Integration

Story isn't placed on top of gameplay — mechanics reinforce the same themes the narrative carries.

Case Study — Tides of Eternity

The problem. The narrative team had no shared framework — every writer worked from a different mental model of the world. What I built. A 14-phase documentation architecture — pillars, world history, faction structure, character psychology, all the way to production priorities. The result. 2-3 narrative designers now work from the same framework — same vocabulary, same pillars, same production logic. The impact. Faster onboarding, fewer contradictions caught late, and a narrative department that can scale past one person.

How I think

Most of this job happens before anyone writes a line of dialogue. The player assembles the story in their own head, out of whatever you leave lying around for them.

I'm suspicious of characters who explain themselves. The ones that land are the ones who contradict themselves and never notice.

A world feels alive when every detail has a reason to be there. More lore is not the same thing as more world.

I got into this because of a rabbit. Rocky was supposed to be a small platformer script and turned into a full bible about a country lying to itself. I've been chasing that feeling since.
